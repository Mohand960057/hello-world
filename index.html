FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does OP Property Management do?

OP Property Management is a business services company that offers four services: short-term rental property management on Booking.com, Airbnb, Agoda and Gathern; HubSpot CRM implementation from A to Z; trademark registration in Saudi Arabia; and online store setup on Noon, Amazon and Trendyol.

Which countries do you serve?

We serve clients in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman and the United States. HubSpot implementation and Amazon store setup are delivered fully remotely.

Which booking platforms do you manage?

We manage vacation rental listings on Booking.com, Airbnb, Agoda and Gathern, including listing setup, pricing, calendars, guest messages and reviews.

What do I need to register a trademark in Saudi Arabia?

Usually your brand name or logo, the owner's details (for companies, the commercial registration) and the list of goods or services the mark will cover. We confirm the exact documents for your case and handle the filing and follow-up with the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP).

Can you set up my store on Noon, Amazon and Trendyol?

Yes. We create and verify your seller account, upload your products with photos, titles and descriptions, set prices and stock, and launch the store.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of each project. Contact us for a free consultation and we will send you a clear, fixed quote.
